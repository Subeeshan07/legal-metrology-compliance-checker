Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Statutory Provisions, Mandatory Declarations & Legal Penalties

Under Section 18 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and Rule 6 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every pre-packaged commodity distributed, sold, or offered for retail sale in India must display clear, unambiguous, and mandatory statutory declarations on its Principal Display Panel.
